# Andiwe Art Website

A mobile-friendly static website for Andiwe Art.

## Before publishing
1. Replace `YOUR-DOMAIN.example` in `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` with your real domain.
2. Confirm the product prices and stock.
3. Replace/add product photos as your real stock becomes available.
4. Publish the site using GitHub Pages, another static host, or your own hosting.
5. Add and verify the site in Google Search Console and request indexing.
6. Create/verify an eligible Google Business Profile so the business information can appear on Google Search and Maps.

The website includes:
- WhatsApp ordering
- Phone contact
- In-person address
- Product prices
- Mobile-friendly design
- SEO metadata
- LocalBusiness structured data
